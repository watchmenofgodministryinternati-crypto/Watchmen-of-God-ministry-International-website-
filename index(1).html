Who We Are

Faith, prayer, and the Gospel

Serving people and sharing the message of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To spread the gospel with boldness, raise prayerful watchmen, and disciple believers—until the message of Christ reaches nations.

Our Vision

To take the Gospel to all nations; to see lives transformed through the Word of God, communities strengthened in faith, and many drawn to Jesus Christ.
